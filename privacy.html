1. Who we are

This website, web-application-developer.com, is run by Web Application Developer LLC, a limited liability company organised in Alaska, United States (“we”, “us”). We decide how and why the personal information described here is used, which makes us its controller under European data protection law.

You can reach us about anything in this policy at hello@web-application-developer.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and any email you exchange with us, including email about testing our software.

It does not cover our apps. Our desktop app keeps what it reads on your own computer, and it says inside the app what it keeps and what, if anything, leaves your computer. When an app is offered to the public, it will come with its own privacy policy, linked from here.

3. What we collect, and why

When you visit this website

We collect nothing ourselves. The site has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no embedded content from other companies.

The site is hosted by GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and basic request details (such as the page asked for, the time, and your browser's user agent) in order to deliver the page, and GitHub may log them to keep its service secure. We cannot see these logs and do not receive them.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, the content of your message and anything you attach, and the technical details any email carries (such as the time it was sent). If you take part in testing our software, this can include reports or files you choose to send us about how the app worked for you.

We use this only to read and answer your message, to run the testing you agreed to take part in, to improve our software from what you tell us, and to keep a record of our correspondence where we need one for legal or accounting reasons.

We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Our legal bases (EU, EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR and UK GDPR require us to tell you why the law allows us to use your information:

  • Answering you and running testing: taking steps at your request, or carrying out an arrangement with you (Article 6(1)(b)), and otherwise our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us and in improving our software (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Keeping records we are required to keep: complying with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Keeping the website secure and available, through our host's server logs: our legitimate interest in running a safe website (Article 6(1)(f)).

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you have the right to object (see Your rights).

6. Where it is stored

We are based in the United States, and both providers above process data in the United States. If you write to us from outside the United States, your information will be transferred there. Where European or UK law applies, we rely on the provider's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension) where it has one, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful safeguard. You can ask us for details of the safeguard that applies.

7. How long we keep it

  • Email: for as long as the conversation or testing is going on, and then for up to 24 months, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or we need to keep it longer to meet a legal obligation or to establish or defend a legal claim.
  • Reports and files from testing: until we no longer need them to improve the software, and no longer than 24 months after the testing they came from ends, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Server logs: kept by GitHub under its own policy. We do not hold them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • find out whether we hold personal information about you, and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • have it deleted;
  • have its use restricted while a question about it is settled;
  • receive what you gave us in a portable, machine-readable form;
  • object to our using it on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what was done before.

To use any of these, email hello@web-application-developer.com. We will answer within one month (or within 45 days where US state law sets that period), and tell you if we need longer and why. We may ask you to confirm you are the person the information is about, usually by replying from the email address we hold. Using your rights is free.

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first, but you do not have to come to us before you complain.

Because we handle so little personal information, and only occasionally, we have not appointed a representative in the EU or UK.

9. For people in the United States

California's Consumer Privacy Act and the privacy laws of other states, such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah and Texas, give their residents rights over personal information. We describe our practices here so that they are clear wherever you live, whether or not a particular law applies to a company of our size.

  • What we collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (your email address and, if you gave it, your name), the content of messages you sent us, and internet or network activity in the form of server request details handled by our host. The sources are you, and your browser when it asks for a page.
  • Why: the purposes in section 3.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not ask for it. If you choose to include it in a message, we use it only to answer you.
  • Selling and sharing: we do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have not done either in the last 12 months. That includes information about anyone under 16.
  • Your rights: to know what we hold and how we use it, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling (none of which we do). You can use them as described in section 8, or through an authorised agent, who will need your signed permission. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our answer, and we will respond to the appeal within the time your state's law sets.
  • No discrimination: we will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.
  • Global Privacy Control: there is nothing on this site for it to opt you out of, but we treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out request.

10. Cookies, tracking and Do Not Track

This website does not use cookies, local storage, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or analytics of any kind, and it loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies. No third party can track you across other websites through this site. Because there is no tracking to switch off, Do Not Track and similar browser signals make no difference to how this site behaves.

11. Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU, EEA and UK). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Our email account is protected with a strong password and two-step sign-in, and access to it is limited to the people who need it. No method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, but we hold very little, and that is the best protection we have.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and change the “Last updated” date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already hold about you, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.
